# Credits: Pokémon Canvas

This hack is built on community work. Thank you to everyone below.

- **pret** — the pokefirered/pokeemerald decompilations: https://github.com/pret
- **Rom Hacking Hideout (RHH)** — pokeemerald-expansion, the engine this hack is built on:
  https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion
- **pret's poketcg** — the Pokémon Trading Card Game disassembly, the source of the Trading Card
  Game's rules, AI, cards, text and graphics: https://github.com/pret/poketcg
- **Pokémon Heart & Soul (HnS)** — the Johto maps, tilesets and tileset animations
  (pokehns-expansion): https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokehns-expansion — see their
  CREDITS.md for the artists and contributors behind them. Also the gyms used as the convention's
  halls, Janine's sprites, and the HeartGold/SoulSilver music (MIDI arrangements and the HGSS
  instrument set).
- **Pokémon Emerald Rogue** (Pokabbie) — the Gym Leader Convention's trainer sprites (front pictures
  and overworld sprites of the later generations' leaders):
  https://github.com/Pokabbie/pokeemerald-rogue — see its credits for the sprite artists.
- **The new eeveelutions' front sprites** — from a community "eeveelutions of every type" fan
  chart. Valyreon is **Kirineon** by **Veterans** (rebornevo.com); Hypereon is **Nimbeon** by
  **PansygumPKMN** (DeviantArt). The other eight are from the same chart, artists not yet
  identified. Edited for the hack: the Normal one recoloured, glasses on the Bug one, mechanical
  details on the Steel one. Their back sprites were adapted from
  AI-generated reference pictures (Google's Nano Banana, made by Nick) and, for Obsideon and
  Subterraneon, from their own fronts; their icons and shiny colours were made for the hack.
